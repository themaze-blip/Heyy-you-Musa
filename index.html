About Him

Some people are easy to describe by what they do. Others are better described by the person they are becoming. Musa is a little bit of both.

This year wasn’t necessarily the easiest year, and not everything went according to plan. But somewhere between the things he was figuring out, the people he was navigating, the responsibilities he was carrying, and the little moments in between, he kept becoming.

He’s funny sometimes without even trying. He’s sweet, loving, and has a way of making people feel cared for. He’s the kind of person who makes time, who can be in your corner, who brings a little lightness into serious moments, and who reminds you that life doesn’t always have to be so heavy.

And beyond all that, he’s a person with things he’s still building, dreams he’s still pursuing, and a purpose he’s still growing into.

This isn’t a celebration of someone who has everything figured out. It’s a celebration of Musa the person, the process, the growth, and all the little things that make him him.

His Journey This YearA Year of Becoming

The year actually started on a pretty good note. He took on STC and even walked away with a distinction, which was already something worth celebrating. Things were going well until life became a little more complicated than expected.

There were disappointments, moments that didn’t quite work out the way he hoped, and seasons that probably asked more of him than he anticipated. But he kept going. He pushed through, found ways to keep himself occupied, tutored others, and continued showing up even when things weren’t necessarily easy.

His cooking journey wasn’t new either. It was something he had started the year before, but this year he kept building on it. He grew more confident in what he could do, continued feeding people, took on opportunities to cook for others and turned something he loved into something people were willing to pay him for.

And somewhere in between everything else, he kept finding his footing. Not necessarily by having everything figured out, but by continuing to move forward. Maybe that is what this year was really about. Not getting everything right, but not giving up on yourself either.

Our FriendshipSomewhere Along the Way

I think somewhere along the way, things became a little different between us. We didn’t stop caring or stop being friends. We just weren’t as present in each other’s lives as we once were.

There were still conversations, little catch ups, moments of sharing what was going on, studying together and the occasional side quest. We still found our way into each other’s days, even if it wasn’t as often as before.

I think that’s something I have come to appreciate about friendships. They don’t always look the same in every season. Sometimes life gets busy, sometimes things shift, and sometimes you don’t realise how much time has passed until you look back.

But even in the quieter seasons, there are people you still wish well, people you still root for and people whose lives you are still glad to be a part of. I’m glad you’re one of those people, Musa. And regardless of how this year looked for us, I will always be rooting for you, celebrating you and hoping life brings you good things.
